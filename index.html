About the developer

Hi, I'm Zohaib Majeed

I am a student of Electrical Engineering at UET New Campus, Kala Shah Kaku, and a proud member of the Batch 2026. Alongside my engineering degree, I am a self-taught web developer.

Nobody handed me a course or a curriculum. I learned web design and development on my own, through online tutorials, documentation and a lot of practice, building small projects again and again until they worked. Every mistake taught me something, and that habit of learning by doing is how this website was made.

I built this website for my class, for our CR Azher Kashif and GR Kashaf Malik, and for every classmate, so that our batch has a proper home on the internet where our names, our campus and our memories live together.

I want to keep growing as both an engineer and a developer, and to build useful things for the people around me. If you would like a website for a society, a project or an idea, feel free to contact me.

Ground & sports

The campus ground is where the batch comes alive

After lectures, the ground fills up. Practice sessions, friendly matches and class tournaments make it the best break of the day.

  • Cricket ground with practice nets
  • Football ground
  • Volleyball and basketball courts
  • Indoor badminton and table tennis
  • Gymnasium
  • Flood lights for evening games
Cafe & canteen

Tea, samosas and the real assignment discussion

The canteen is where friendships start. Between lectures it turns into a meeting room, a study corner and a place to simply relax with friends.

  • Tea, snacks and full meals
  • Open seating for group discussions
  • The unofficial meeting point of every class
Library & labs

A calm place to learn

For assignments, exam preparation and hands-on practical work, the library and laboratories give us the space and the equipment to focus.

  • Peaceful library for reading and revision
  • Air-conditioned classroom blocks
  • Smart boards and modern lab equipment
